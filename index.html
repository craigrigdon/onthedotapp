iPhone app

Leave on time
Arrive on time.

On The Dot tells you when to leave — not just when to arrive. It watches travel time from where you are, then reminds you to go.

What it does

Missions

Save a destination and an arrive-by time. On The Dot calculates leave time from live travel estimates.

Leave alerts

Countdown notifications and optional Live Activities so you actually walk out the door.

Background location

Departure times and ETAs stay current while you’re on the move — used for leave times, not ads.

Shared groups

Invite family or teammates to the same missions. Sharing is opt-in; only people you invite see the group.

Traffic & Back to Car

A traffic recheck about 30 minutes before leave, plus a parked-car pin when you need to find the car again.

Widgets & car apps

Glance at leave time on the Home Screen, and send a destination to Tesla or Rivian when you choose to.

Free

Missions for everyone

Core missions, leave alerts, and group sharing are available to everyone. No account to create — you use your Apple ID.

Premium

When the week is full

Monthly, annual, or lifetime (with a trial on subscriptions). Purchases go through Apple. We never see your card number.

Privacy

Your data stays with you

Missions sync with your iCloud account. We do not run a separate On The Dot server for your trips. Analytics and punctuality predictions are built on your device from your own history.
